# Abstract {.unnumbered}

Two complete-intersection Calabi–Yau threefolds in the CIPro archive have the same Hodge numbers and four cubic and Chern divisibilities. All ambient line-bundle indices on one are even, and a hyperplane bundle on the other has index $5$. We obtain the gcd over the entire ambient lattice by finite differences of the Riemann–Roch polynomial. It refines the {{REFINEMENT_QUADRUPLES}} earlier joint signatures to {{INDEX_DIVISIBILITY_SIGNATURES}} across {{CICY3_NONPRODUCT_ROWS}} non-product configurations. For two other threefolds of Picard rank one, a difference in the cubic on primitive generators rules out a homeomorphism. We reproduce the Euler characteristics and {{CHERN_AMBIENT_PAIRINGS}} second Chern pairings for the pinned catalogue of {{CICY3_RECORDS}} presentations.

The relative frequency of a Hodge pair in that catalogue depends on the number of times it is presented. Giving each non-product Hodge pair equal total weight shifts the median Euler characteristic from {{ATLAS_PRESENTATION_MEDIAN}} to {{ATLAS_PAIR_MEDIAN}}. We also compute all four line-bundle cohomology dimensions on a specified smooth bicubic by rational multiplication-map ranks. At {{BICUBIC_EXACT_JUMPING_POINTS}} of {{BICUBIC_EXACT_POINTS}} charges, the dimensions differ from the generic benchmark while the index agrees. These examples identify when a comparison requires the archive weights, the divisor lattice, or the defining equation. py-CIPro supplies the native calculations, source records, and figure generators.
