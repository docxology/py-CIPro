# Abstract {.unnumbered}

py-CIPro brings the released CIPro geometry package to Python through native exact arithmetic, a callable Wolfram Language path, and an interactive catalogue. We use the pinned release to study how computational results depend on their inputs. First, we independently recover the Euler characteristics and {{CHERN_AMBIENT_PAIRINGS}} second Chern pairings of all {{CICY3_RECORDS}} archived configurations. At the modal Hodge pair, a fixed rule for choosing an ample line bundle gives {{CHERN_MODAL_SIGNATURES}} distinct joint values of polarized degree and second Chern pairing. Second, weighting each non-product Hodge pair equally moves the median Euler characteristic from {{ATLAS_PRESENTATION_MEDIAN}} to {{ATLAS_PAIR_MEDIAN}}. An exact decomposition explains the resulting change in the weighted share of records carrying the released favorability flag. Third, rational multiplication-map ranks give all four line-bundle cohomology dimensions on a specified smooth bicubic at {{BICUBIC_EXACT_POINTS}} charges. Its dimensions differ from the generic benchmark at {{BICUBIC_EXACT_JUMPING_POINTS}} charges while every index agrees. The analyses, figures, and verification receipts are reproducible from pinned inputs with Python.
